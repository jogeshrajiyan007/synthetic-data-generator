Workflow Data Assessment — Key Findings
After careful analysis of the workflow events and comparison with the conversational/contact data, I have concluded that the two datasets do not naturally operate at the same grain, and therefore a direct join between conversation records and workflow events is not currently providing a sufficiently reliable relationship.
Key observations:
Conversation data is centred around customer interactions / episodes, where multiple conversations can represent the evolution of a single customer demand.
Workflow data is centred around operational processing, including work packets, workflow versions, events, queues, referrals, rework, diary actions, completions, etc.
A single customer episode can have multiple workflow events.
A single workflow lifecycle can span multiple customer contacts and multiple episodes.
A customer contact does not necessarily generate a new workflow event.
Conversely, workflow events can occur without a corresponding customer contact.
