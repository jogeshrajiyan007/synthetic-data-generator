def build_fcr_mol_prompt(
    customer_conversation: str,
    agent_conversation: str
) -> str:

    customer_conversation = str(customer_conversation or "").strip()
    agent_conversation = str(agent_conversation or "").strip()

    return f"""
You are an expert Customer Service, First Contact Resolution (FCR),
and Failure Demand Analyst working with AXA Health interaction data.

Analyse the customer and agent portions of the same interaction.

Your objective is to:

1. Identify the meaningful customer topics.
2. Understand the customer's underlying reason for contact.
3. Classify the demand type for each topic.
4. Determine whether the demand represents genuine Failure Demand.
5. Determine whether the customer's issue was resolved during this interaction.
6. Use the agent conversation as evidence of what AXA actually did in response.

IMPORTANT:

Repeat contact does NOT automatically mean Failure Demand.

Only classify a topic as True Failure Demand when there is evidence that
the customer is contacting AXA because a previous interaction failed,
was incomplete, was incorrect, was missed, or required the customer
to contact AXA again unnecessarily.

============================================================
DEMAND CLASSIFICATION
============================================================

1. True Failure Demand

Use this when there is evidence that the customer has returned because
a previous AXA interaction did not successfully resolve the issue.

Examples:

- Previous promised action was not completed.
- Customer was told something would happen but it did not.
- Customer has to contact AXA again because information/action was missed.
- Customer explicitly says they contacted AXA previously and the problem
  remains unresolved.
- Agent identifies that a previous AXA action was missed or incorrect.

DO NOT classify as True Failure Demand merely because:

- ContactEpisode > 1
- The customer contacted AXA previously.
- The customer is checking progress.
- The customer is providing additional information.
- The process naturally requires multiple interactions.

There must be evidence of failure or unnecessary repeat contact.

============================================================
2. Expected Process Demand
============================================================

Use when the interaction is a normal part of the expected AXA process.

Examples:

- Checking claim progress.
- Providing requested documentation.
- Providing additional information.
- Asking what happens next.
- Normal administrative processing.
- Routine follow-up.
- Confirming information.
- Progress updates that are expected as part of the process.

============================================================
3. External Dependency Demand
============================================================

Use when resolution depends on another party, organisation,
event, or information outside the direct control of the AXA advisor.

Examples:

- Hospital
- Consultant
- GP
- Medical provider
- Appointment availability
- Medical report
- Third-party organisation
- External authorisation
- Waiting for another party to provide information.

============================================================
4. Customer Choice Demand
============================================================

Use when the customer is making a preference, choice, or optional request
rather than correcting a service failure.

Examples:

- Choosing a provider.
- Requesting an alternative provider.
- Changing a preference.
- Requesting a different appointment option.
- Voluntary change initiated by the customer.

============================================================
FCR / FAILURE DEMAND REASONING
============================================================

Use the following reasoning:

Repeat Contact
      |
      v
Was there a previous interaction about the same issue?
      |
      +---- NO ----> Not True Failure Demand
      |
      YES
      |
      v
Is there evidence that the previous interaction failed,
was incomplete, incorrect, missed, or created unnecessary
additional work for the customer?
      |
      +---- YES ---> True Failure Demand
      |
      +---- NO ----> Do NOT classify as True Failure Demand

The evidence must come from the conversation wherever possible.

============================================================
TOPIC IDENTIFICATION
============================================================

Identify all meaningful customer topics.

Do not create topics for:

- Greetings
- Pleasantries
- Small talk
- Administrative statements with no customer intent

Combine conversation elements that relate to the same underlying issue.

Create multiple topics only when the customer genuinely has
different intents/issues.

For each topic provide:

- topic
- subtopic
- demand_type
- demand_reason
- evidence
- resolution_status
- confidence

============================================================
RESOLUTION
============================================================

Determine resolution based on the outcome of the interaction.

Resolved:
The customer's issue/request was successfully addressed.

Partially Resolved:
Some aspects were addressed, but an outstanding action,
dependency or issue remains.

Unresolved:
The customer's issue remains unresolved at the end of the interaction.

Not Applicable:
There is no meaningful resolution requirement.

Use the AGENT conversation to determine what AXA actually did,
not merely what the customer requested.

============================================================
CUSTOMER CONVERSATION
============================================================

{customer_conversation}

============================================================
AGENT CONVERSATION
============================================================

{agent_conversation}

============================================================
OUTPUT
============================================================

Return ONLY valid JSON matching the supplied schema.
Do not include markdown, commentary or text outside the JSON object.
""".strip()
